# Orientation Nudge Template

## Campaign: incomplete_orientation
## Variables: {first_name}, {num_modules_completed}, {company}

Variants for partners who started the In-App Orientation and stalled. Variant A leans on the progress they've already made; Variant B adds a **Reply HELP** keyword CTA for partners who may be stuck. Variant C covers both-deal zones where the partner chose a company and stalled; Variant D covers both-deal zones where the partner engaged but never chose. No em dashes in any partner-facing copy. Keyword behavior: see `_config/response_playbook/keyword_replies.md`.

## Variant A — Progress Emphasis

Hey {first_name}, you're partway through the In-app orientation. Almost there! The orientation has 3 steps; finish the learning modules plus the certification call and background check to earn $10 and unlock shifts in your area.

Pick up where you left off: shiftsmart://explore

Need help with anything? Just reply.

## Variant B — Progress + Reply HELP CTA

Hey {first_name}, you've made progress on the In-app orientation. A few more minutes plus a short certification call and background check (step 3) and you'll have $10 and your shifts unlocked.

Reply **HELP** if you're stuck on anything, or pick up here: shiftsmart://explore

## Variant C — Both-Deal Zone, company chosen, stalled before or during orientation

Hey {first_name}, the {company} orientation is still waiting for you. It only takes {duration: "about 45 minutes" for Circle K, "about 20 to 30 minutes" for Dollar General} on your phone and progress saves automatically. Finish it to earn $10 and unlock shifts near you.

Pick up where you left off: shiftsmart://explore

Need help with anything? Just reply.

## Variant D — Both-Deal Zone, engaged but never chose a company

Still here to help when you're ready! Quick recap: Circle K is food prep and stocking coolers (orientation about 45 min), Dollar General is organizing shelves and updating price tags (about 20 to 30 min). Both pay $10 when you finish.
